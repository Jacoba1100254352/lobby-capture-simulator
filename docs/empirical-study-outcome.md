# Empirical study outcome for the current evidence freeze

Assessment date: September 13, 2026. This synthesis covers the committed
procurement, substitution and comment studies. It introduces no new observations
or estimates. The source ledgers and scoped reviews linked below remain the
authoritative evidence for individual findings.

The current evidence does not identify a causal procurement-capture effect,
cross-channel substitution effect or individual comment effect. This is a
negative identification result, not an estimate that those effects are zero.
The studies also establish narrower documentary findings. In particular, an
agency-documented comment response corresponds to a verified published model-input
revision, and historical procurement pages strengthen a selected award linkage.

**The full empirical objective remains unfinished.** A representative SAM export
has not been obtained and validated. Historical exclusion coverage and a credible
substitution comparison remain unresolved. This report documents the result of
the available evidence; it does not replace those acquisition and design
requirements with a smaller completion standard.

## Procurement: substantial transaction coverage, incomplete outcome linkage

The declared acquisition population is fiscal year 2024 across twelve agencies.
The archived USAspending profile contains 6,449,101 transaction rows with a
366-day planned coverage interval for each agency. These are not 6,449,101 unique
awards or a representative SAM export. The archive mixes child-award descriptions
and blank award types; a reviewed blank-type record is an IDV. There are
243,700 blank award-type rows and 4,505,798 rows without an offer count. The 351
explicit zero-offer rows remain distinct from missing counts. The separate
28,103-row ranked panel is a selected sample, without established probability
sampling weights.

The source-definition repair distinguishes signed action dates from approval or
performance dates, action obligations from cumulative totals, and action-level
offer counts from parent counts. Competition after exclusion of sources does not
supply historical vendor-exclusion status. These distinctions are implemented and
tested, but a matched SAM/USAspending comparison has not been completed.

The selected GAO follow-up links four original award actions in the archived bulk
data. A June 2024 VA page corroborates all four service-area labels. The sixteen
provisional award/docket pairs cover thirteen dockets in one consolidated decision
family; they are not sixteen independent protests or a population denominator.
Later publisher-page conflicts, missing solicitation identifiers and an unresolved
four-offers/five-proposals difference prevent stronger timing and count claims.
No exact award-specific protest or stay interval, historical exclusion interval,
or exclusion absence is inferred.

A separate reconciliation matches all 34,657 rows across thirteen fields between
the saved Agriculture quarter and its alternative partitions. This rules out a
disagreement between those saved exports. It does not explain the planning count's
twenty-one-row excess, identify missing records or justify appending overlapping
partitions.

**Result:** the evidence supports transaction-frame diagnostics and a selected
documentary linkage. It does not support representative protest/exclusion rates,
a SAM coding-agreement rate, or a causal procurement-capture estimate.
See [procurement source reconciliation](procurement-source-reconciliation.md)
for field definitions, archive identities, exact source locations and access limits.

## Substitution: longer history does not resolve treatment and measurement

The expanded LDA source frame has 427 filing UUIDs and 1,119 issue rows attributed
by the API to six candidates over 2003-2008. Each candidate has nine nominal
pre-enactment native reporting periods. Issue rows can repeat filing-level amounts;
native-period presence does not establish complete filings, final amendments,
correct actor attribution or comparable spending. Income, organizational expenses
and different expense-accounting methods cannot be added as one common outcome.

The 72 actor-half-years contain 36 same-candidate LDA/PAC source overlaps. Twelve
expected PAC periods remain unresolved, and 24 actor-half-years are outside the
four-PAC acquisition cohort. Those gaps remain visible rather than becoming
zero alternate-channel spending. Splitting semiannual reports into quarters
would not create additional independent pre-reform observations.

The separate legacy comparison contains 231 included actor-quarters. In a model
that includes both treatment-by-post and federal-source-by-post, those two columns
are identical and the five-column design has rank four. Opposite changes in their
coefficients leave predictions unchanged. This establishes non-identification for
that augmented comparison; it does not establish that a source-specific shock
actually occurred or that every comparison across reporting systems is invalid.

**Result:** no successor causal substitution estimate is fitted for this freeze.
Reopening requires dated provision-specific pre-reform exposure and a credible
comparison, followed by resolved source versions, comparable accounting measures,
historical actor/PAC links and common outcome coverage. Quarterly reporting,
gift/travel restrictions and coalition disclosure require separate exposure and
timing definitions. See the [substitution redesign](substitution-study-redesign.md)
and its linked comparison report for the exact rank diagnostic and availability
table. Neither a null reform effect nor a substitution elasticity is estimated.

## Comments: documented response and input uptake, without a causal rate

All 48 segmented requests in one MEMA publisher letter have a bounded first-pass
review. The letter was selected after source and outcome exposure. Its requests
are not independent comments or policy events, and unresolved instruments remain
unresolved. Original docket-byte identity and independent adjudication are still
missing. The separate Utah corpus and other purposive pilots do not supply a
common denominator for this letter.

For the existing concrete-mixer PTO request, EPA reports using MEMA's submitted
35-49% range, and the final RIA publishes its 42% midpoint. The draft RIA and native
proposal workbook assign the scoped mixer vehicle 25%. This is a verified
17-percentage-point published input revision. It is not an estimated change in
total fuel use, emissions, adoption, lobbying power or the comment's causal effect.

The proposal workbook's stored calculation multiplies driving fuel by 25% to
obtain PTO fuel, making PTO 20% of those two stored fuel components. This scoped
arithmetic does not validate the empirical denominator of MEMA's range. The final
model and output-calculator workbooks remain unread, so the proposal formula is
not extrapolated to an executed final-model result.

**Result:** the evidence supports source-linked documentary coding and a published
input change. It does not support a representative uptake rate or an individual
influence estimate. See the [comment-response review](comment-response-document-pilot.md)
for the 48-entry reading index, source/version boundaries and native calculation.

## Evidence that would change the result

| Unfinished requirement | Evidence needed before stronger analysis |
| --- | --- |
| Representative SAM export and source reconciliation | An accessible export for the prespecified agency/time population, including original actions and modifications, with action dates, obligations, identifiers and offer-count provenance; then matched and unmatched coverage and field disagreements by agency. Screening thresholds alone do not establish representativeness. |
| Historical protest/exclusion linkage | Source-specific solicitation/award identity, timing and coverage; public exclusion records with interpretable snapshot/revision history and adjudicated UEI/date intervals. A current active-only list or a missing match cannot establish historical absence. |
| Redesigned substitution comparison | Dated pre-reform exposure, a credible comparison, resolved filing versions and accounting definitions, historical actor/PAC links and comparable alternate-channel outcomes over a common period. More filing rows alone do not identify the effect. |
| Stronger comment-uptake measurement | A declared sampling frame, verified original versions and independent review of segmentation and response/disposition links. Tracing the final PTO calculation additionally requires the final workbook; obtaining it alone would not identify a causal comment effect. |

The Java model remains a stylized mechanism model. These source products have not
recalibrated its policy parameters or validated real-world reform rankings.
Reproducibility checks establish consistency of the archived analysis and packaged
evidence, not independent source adjudication or empirical effect identification.
